# Impeccable Design & Craft Guidelines

## Design Standards
1. **Refined Dark Mode**: Use rich slate, zinc, and neutral dark palettes with subtle luminosity rather than harsh solid black.
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Typography**: Maintain deliberate font scale ratios, crisp tracking, and balanced line heights. Avoid unstyled fallback states.
3. **Glassmorphism & Depth**: Utilize layered borders (`border-slate-800/80`), subtle backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur-md`), and localized glows to convey elevation and hierarchy.
4. **Micro-Interactions**: Provide smooth transitions on hover, active, and focus states with reactive feedback.
5. **No Placeholders**: Ensure all components and cards have complete, well-typeset content and icons.
